Beta Ideas for further analysis

These analyses are outside the scope of the research. They are built on the same recorded runs and work like the other pages, but treat the findings as exploratory: they are not claims of the paper.

Does a busy server explain the slow calls?

Every LLM call, grouped by how many calls were in flight when it started, itself included. Dots are medians, lines the interquartile range. Latency mixes short and long answers, so time per output token is the cleaner measure of contention.

Who talks to whom, and how fast they agree

Left: Full mesh discussion messages by sender and receiver role. Right: of the discussions that reached each round, the share that ended there with every agent agreeing (Star: every reviewer approving the solver's proposal).

How the prompt grows across discussion rounds

Prompt tokens per discussion call by round: median (dot) and interquartile range (bar). Each round carries the previous rounds' history, cut to a fixed length once it grows too long.

How often a run needs another iteration, and what it costs

A run starts again from recruitment when the evaluator does not accept its answer, up to three iterations. Many rejections are replies the evaluator could not score at all.

Does more traffic buy better answers?

Mean evaluation score (with its 95% confidence interval) against the median cost of a run (with its interquartile range). The score is the evaluator's 0 to 100 grade of the final answer.
